About Us

Leave us a message and we'll reply as soon as possible. We're happy to answer any order questions and general inquiries.

Built for Brands. Printed for Impact.

Cups Galore is a proudly Australian-owned and operated business based in Southwest Sydney, dedicated to manufacturing high-quality custom printed coffee and drink cups. Since the very beginning, we have committed ourselves to excellence in every cup we produce, ensuring that each one carries your brand's message directly into the hands of your audience.

We don't just print cups. We support businesses with end-to-end cup manufacturing solutions designed to scale with demand.

  • Precision printing for clear, consistent branding
  • Reliable production timelines and volume capability
  • Quality control at every stage of manufacturing
  • Support for short runs, bulk orders, and repeat programs

Designed for Performance and Sustainability

Sustainability is not an afterthought—it's embedded in how we manufacture. Our paper cups are developed with responsibly sourced materials and production methods that reduce waste without compromising durability or print quality.

We continuously refine our processes to balance:

  • Environmental responsibility
  • Food-safe compliance
  • Structural strength for hot and cold beverages
  • Support for short runs, bulk orders, and repeat programs

What Makes Us Different?

Built on local manufacturing expertise and driven by modern production standards, Cups Galore delivers custom printed cups that balance brand impact, operational reliability, and scale.

Australian Manufacturing Experience

Years of industry-focused production

We have produced millions of custom printed cups for cafés, hospitality groups, and growing brands across Australia. Our experience allows us to support everything from short runs to high-volume repeat orders—without compromising quality.

Advanced Printing & Production Capability

Precision equipment. Consistent results.

Our advanced printing and production capability is built around precision equipment and efficient workflows that ensure consistently high-quality results. Every cup is produced with sharp, accurate printing and the structural strength required for both hot and cold beverages.

Brand-First Design Support

Packaging designed to represent your business

We work closely with brands to ensure designs translate perfectly onto cups—whether you're launching a new identity or rolling out packaging across multiple locations. Every cup is treated as a brand asset, not just packaging.

Reliable, Scalable Partnership

Built for growing businesses

From independent cafés to expanding hospitality operations, we provide manufacturing systems that scale with demand. Our clients value us not just as a supplier—but as a long-term packaging partner.
