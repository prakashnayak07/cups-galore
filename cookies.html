Cookie Policy

This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage them.

Last updated: 16 June 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

  • Essential cookies — required for core site functionality such as your cart and checkout.
  • Performance cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • Functional cookies — remember your preferences for a smoother experience.
  • Marketing cookies — used, with your consent, to show relevant content and offers.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of the website, including the shopping cart and checkout.

4. Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us.
