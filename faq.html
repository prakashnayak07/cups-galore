Artwork Guidelines

Please ensure that all images are embedded and fonts are outlined. Your image must fit inside the image area (the red dashed lines).

Provide plenty of bleed if the background is patterned or coloured.

Leave the template flat as we use specialised software to warp it and generate a 3D visual for your final approval.

Save the file in hi-res pdf format (without flattening the guide lines as part of the design).

Please provide your artwork in CMYK.
